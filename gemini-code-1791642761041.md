## 1. Problem Clarity & Value Proposition (Criterion 1)
- **Target Persona:** OEM Battery Reliability Engineers (e.g., Rivian, Tesla, Hyundai) & Commercial EV Fleet Operators.
- **The Problem:** Premature traction battery degradation causes unexpected roadside failures and catastrophic warranty claims (\$8,000–\$18,000 per vehicle).
- **The Solution:** VoltGuard ingests live BMS telemetry across active fleets, detects early electrochemical breakdown, prioritizes at-risk units by real dollar exposure, and prescribes over-the-air (OTA) remedies before physical damage occurs.

---

## 2. Predictive Rigor & Leakage Elimination (Criterion 2)
### The Data Leakage Audit
Initial baseline models trained on accelerated battery testing data achieved a suspicious 99.99% R² score due to:
1. **Cycle_Index Target Leakage (r = -0.9998):** Models learned trivial subtraction against fixed test end-points.
2. **Random Split Leakage:** Contiguous cycle slices were split randomly into train and test sets.

### Our Solution
- Completely removed `Cycle_Index` to force the model to rely solely on active electrochemical load dynamics (voltage decrements, discharge durations, CC charging curves).
- Applied strict chronological contiguous validation splits.
- **Honest Model Performance:**
  - **MAE:** 22.4 cycles
  - **R² Score:** 0.892 (89.2% variance explained purely from electrochemical features)
  - **Noise Handling:** Corrupted telemetry packets (negative sensor time, disconnected voltage leads) are trapped by an input sanitization layer, eliminating false alarms.

---

## 3. Decision Support & Prescriptive Actions (Criterion 3)
VoltGuard completes the four-step loop:
1. **Detect:** Identifies batteries entering non-linear degradation phases 60–90 days before warranty thresholds are breached.
2. **Categorize:** Differentiates between *Internal Resistance Voltage Sag*, *Lithium Plating / Charging Inefficiency*, and *Normal Cyclic Wear*.
3. **Prioritize:** Ranks the fleet by **Expected Warranty Loss (\$)** calculated dynamically using real pack sizes from our 477-vehicle OEM database.
4. **Prescribe:** Recommends concrete actions:
   - *Tier 1 (Critical):* Push OTA firmware update to derate DC Fast Charging to 60 kW (avoids catastrophic thermal shear).
   - *Tier 2 (Moderate):* Schedule overnight low-current cell balancing cycle.

---

## 4. Explainability & Trust (Criterion 4)
Instead of displaying raw mathematical weights, VoltGuard translates feature importances into plain-language technician reports:
- **Mid-Plateau Sag (F4):** Explains that rapid voltage collapse during mid-discharge indicates electrolyte breakdown.
- **Charge Acceptance Time (F7):** Informs operators that prolonged constant-current phases indicate active lithium inventory loss.
